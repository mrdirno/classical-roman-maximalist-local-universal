## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene where a character uses a seemingly mundane object (like a seal, a tile, a specific food) to deduce a complex political or financial truth.
- Explore a moment of silent communication or understanding between two characters from vastly different social strata (e.g., a senator and his litter-bearer, a matron and her Greek physician).
- Detail the ritual of a minor, household religious observance and show how it is being weaponized for social or economic pressure.
- Write a dialogue where every line is a polite, coded lie, and the true conflict simmers beneath the formal language.

- Build an interactive map of the Roman Forum, keyed to the novel's chapters, allowing users to click on locations (Basilica Aemilia, a specific stall) to read excerpts and see character paths.
